A LITTLE MORE ABOUT THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Oh, hello. I’m Fern.

A soft heart, a restless mind, and a fondness for the extraordinary.

I'm someone who feels deeply and thinks just as deeply. I notice the little things: a pattern others pass over, a detail that changes everything, or a small living creature that deserves gentleness. I'm fiercely independent, endlessly curious, and perhaps a little unconventional. Compassion and complexity have always lived side by side in me.

I'm also a designer, developer, and student of AI software engineering and computer science, with hands-on experience leading businesses. I love untangling difficult problems, understanding how things connect, and imagining possibilities that aren't immediately obvious. To me, intelligence isn't only about what you can build. It's also about how thoughtfully you use it.

The luna moth feels like a reflection of me: delicate, resilient, drawn toward light, and beautiful even against darkness. I bring that same contrast to my work — analytical precision with imagination, warmth, and care. I want what I create to work beautifully, of course. But more than that, I want people to feel understood.
