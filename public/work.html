FOLLOW THE CURIOSITY

Oh, hello. I’m Fern.

Curiouser and curiouser… the best ideas rarely follow a straight path.

I'm Fern, an independent designer and developer based in Norfolk, Virginia. I've always been the sort to follow an interesting idea down the rabbit hole — and see what wonderful, unexpected thing might be waiting on the other side.

With a background in business and studies in AI software engineering and computer science, I turn that curiosity into working websites and applications. A little imagination, a little logic, and plenty of attention to the details.

I don't believe every business belongs in the same little box. When we work together, you work directly with me to create something that feels distinctly yours — because ordinary was never the point.
