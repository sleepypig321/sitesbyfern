AN HONEST AMBITION

My mission.

Save the world. Get rich. Help others get rich in a better world.

That's the honest version. I want to build real wealth doing work that excites me. I want the people and businesses I work with to grow, succeed, and build wealth of their own. And I want that success to mean something beyond money.

I know I can't fix every problem in the world by myself. But I can build with intention, treat people with care, and direct a growing share of what this business earns toward the lives, communities, and places that need help. To me, ambition and compassion belong together.

THE LONG VIEW

Success should reach further.

My goal is to build a profitable business that helps clients thrive and gives back as it grows. At the fourth milestone, the giving roadmap reaches a combined 10% of subsequent eligible Stripe payments: 1.5% carbon removal, 3.5% animal welfare, 2.5% volunteer fire and rescue, and 2.5% cleanup.

A note on transparency

This is the giving roadmap for Sites by Fern, not a claim that all four tiers are currently active. Thresholds refer to cumulative Stripe revenue processed by the business. Each new allocation is intended to begin only on eligible payments after its threshold; earlier sales are not charged retroactively. Organizations and the timing of distributions will be identified before each charitable tier begins. Specific recipients and any applicable terms will be published once confirmed.
